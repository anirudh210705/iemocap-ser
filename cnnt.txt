Project: Speaker-independent speech emotion recognition on IEMOCAP

1. FINAL RESULT
---------------
Five-fold Leave-One-Session-Out (LOSO) evaluation was completed for the CNN
baseline. Each of the five IEMOCAP sessions served as the test set exactly once.
The validation session was also held out, and the remaining three sessions were
used for training. No speaker occurred in more than one split within a fold.

Mean test accuracy: 45.64%
Accuracy standard deviation: 3.59 percentage points
Mean test macro-F1: 45.21%
Macro-F1 standard deviation: 2.80 percentage points
Mean test weighted-F1: 43.62%

The recommended headline result for the CNN baseline is therefore:

  Accuracy = 45.64% +/- 3.59%
  Macro-F1 = 45.21% +/- 2.80%

The standard deviations above are sample standard deviations across the five
session-level test folds.

5. CLASS-WISE RESULTS
---------------------
Mean class F1 across the five folds:

angry:   48.13%
happy:   29.14%
neutral: 41.35%
sad:     62.24%

The CNN performed best on sad speech and worst on happy speech. Happy utterances
were frequently confused with other emotions in several sessions. Variation in
the fold scores also confirms that performance depends on the held-out speakers,
which is why the five-fold mean is more reliable than a single fixed split.

6. INTERPRETATION
-----------------
The completed LOSO experiment establishes a proper speaker-independent CNN
baseline. Its mean macro-F1 of 45.21% is higher and more representative than the
earlier single-run Session-5 macro-F1 of 36.72%. The values are not contradictory:
the earlier run was one realization on one held-out session, while LOSO averages
performance across every session and independently selects a checkpoint in each
fold. The fold range (42.71% to 49.59% macro-F1) shows measurable cross-session
variation.

This baseline can now be compared fairly with Wav2Vec2 plus MLP and the frozen-LLM
model, provided those models use these exact same five folds and report the same
mean and standard-deviation metrics.
