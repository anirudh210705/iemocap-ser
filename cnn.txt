1. EXPERIMENT SUMMARY
---------------------
Only the CNN baseline was trained. No Wav2Vec2, HuBERT, or BLOOMZ/LLM model was
started. The model was trained on IEMOCAP Sessions 1-3, selected using Session 4
validation performance, and evaluated once on the untouched Session 5 test set.
This is a speaker-independent split because speakers do not overlap between the
training, validation, and test sessions.

5. INTERPRETATION
-----------------
The CNN provides a completed end-to-end baseline for the project. Its strongest
class F1 scores are for sad (50.11%) and neutral (49.29%). Angry speech has high
recall (69.41%) but low precision, meaning the model over-predicts anger. Happy is
the main weakness: only 22 of 442 happy test utterances were correctly detected,
with most confused as angry or neutral. This demonstrates the limitation of a
small log-Mel CNN on speaker-independent emotion recognition and establishes the
baseline that later pretrained-speech and frozen-LLM experiments should improve.
